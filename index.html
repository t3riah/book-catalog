U.S. Television News and Cold War Propaganda, 1947-1960

Nancy E. Bernhard, 1999

HistoryRead in full

Thesis in short: From about 1948 to 1954, government information officers and young network news divisions jointly produced programs presented as news or public affairs, with government supplying scripts, film, and officials and networks supplying airtime and legitimacy. Open co-production faded in the mid 1950s, but official source dependence and anticommunism built into the neutrality standard stayed.

Coverage: Full main text read Nov 4, 2026. Footnotes used for scope, not summarized line by line.

Politics, Propaganda, and Pop Culture: Cold War Television Cultures

Eds. Christopher Porter, Anthony Salisbury, Tyler Ripley, 2024

HistoryRead in full

Thesis in short: Television did not just report the Cold War. It projected, shaped, and amplified Cold War ideas, but what it could do depended on who owned and controlled production, on technology and staging, and on the audience.

Coverage: All 12 chapters read in full from the Virginia Tech Publishing PDF, Nov 4, 2026. Largest limit: audience effects mostly inferred, not measured.

The Myth of Mental Illness: Foundations of a Theory of Personal Conduct

Thomas Szasz, 1961

PsychologySampled study

Thesis in short: Mental illness is not a disease of the mind in the same sense as a disease of the body, because the mind is not a physical organ that can be diseased in that way. What psychiatry labels mental illness is better understood as problems in living, in communication, and in following or breaking social rules.

Coverage: Sampled study Nov 4, 2026, NOT a full word-for-word read. Preface in part, Introduction, opening spreads of all 16 chapters, Summary opening, Epilogue.

The Gospel of Thomas

Attributed to Didymos Judas Thomas

BibleRead in full

Thesis in short: A collection of 114 secret sayings of the living Jesus, with no narrative, no passion, and no resurrection story. Salvation is finding the interpretation of the sayings, knowing oneself, and becoming one, undivided, so the kingdom, already spread out on the earth and inside a person, is seen.

Coverage: Sayings 1 to 114 read in full Nov 6, 2026, in the Lambdin translation. Lacunae in sayings 60, 61, 93, and 101 are gaps in the manuscript as rendered, not filled in. The Meyer edition introduction and notes were not read.

Man's Search for Meaning

Viktor E. Frankl

Personal GrowthRead in full

Thesis in short: A man can be stripped of everything except one freedom, the freedom to choose his attitude in any given set of circumstances. Life does not ask us what its meaning is; life questions us, and we answer by taking responsibility for the unique task set before us, in work, in love, and in suffering.

Coverage: Contents pp. vii-viii, Preface pp. ix-xv, Part One pp. 3-148, Part Two pp. 149-214, Bibliography pp. 215-222, read in full Nov 6, 2026. Front-matter scans 1-6 not read in full.

Awaken the Giant Within

Anthony Robbins (Tony Robbins)

Personal GrowthSampled study

Thesis in short: Lasting change comes from three decisions: raise your standards, change your limiting beliefs, and change your strategy. Pain and pleasure associations drive everything; change those associations, and the questions, words, metaphors, values, rules, references, and identity behind them, and you change your emotional, physical, relationship, financial, and time destiny.

Coverage: Sampled study Nov 6, 2026, NOT a full word-for-word read. About 160 spreads of about 746 pages in two-page mode. Chapters 1, 19, 21, 23, and 25 read essentially in full; other chapters at opening, central frameworks and exercises, and close.

The Alchemist

Paulo Coelho, translated by Alan R. Clarke

FictionRead in full

Thesis in short: A shepherd boy dreams twice of treasure at the Egyptian Pyramids, leaves everything to follow it, and learns that his Personal Legend is not the gold at the end but the journey itself: listening to omens, learning the Language of the World, loving without ownership, and listening to his heart. The treasure was buried where he started, under the sycamore in the abandoned church.

Coverage: Foreword, Prologue, Part One, Part Two, and Epilogue read in full Nov 6, 2026, 91 percent of the file. Back matter after the Epilogue not read.
