The Gospel of Thomas

Date: Nov 6, 2026

Author: attributed to Didymos Judas Thomas

Thesis in short

A collection of 114 secret sayings of the living Jesus, with no narrative, no passion, and no resurrection story. Salvation is finding the interpretation of the sayings, knowing oneself, and becoming one, undivided, so the kingdom, already spread out on the earth and inside a person, is seen. About two thirds of the sayings have parallels in the canonical gospels; the rest press toward inward knowledge, singleness, and the solitary elect.

Overall summary

Thomas opens: these are the secret sayings which the living Jesus spoke and which Didymos Judas Thomas wrote down. What follows is a list, in no particular order, of short sayings, parables, dialogues, and riddles. Jesus is the living one who gives what eye has not seen, who is the light over all, the all, present in wood and stone. The disciples ask about leadership, prayer, fasting, circumcision, the end, and when the kingdom will come. The answers repeatedly turn outward questions inward: the kingdom is inside you and outside you, it will not come by waiting or by pointing here or there, it is spread out on the earth and men do not see it. The book ends with Peter saying Mary should leave, women are not worthy of life, and Jesus answering that he will lead her to become a living spirit resembling the males.

Sayings, grouped

Sayings 1 to 6: seek and find. Whoever finds the interpretation will not experience death. Seek until troubled, astonished, then rule over the All. The kingdom is not in the sky or the sea, it is inside and outside; know yourselves and you will be known as sons of the living father, fail to know yourselves and you dwell in poverty. An old man will ask a seven day old child about the place of life. First become last, and become one and the same. Recognize what is in sight, and the hidden will become plain. Do not tell lies, do not do what you hate.

Sayings 7 to 13: lion and man, fisherman and large fish, sower, fire, heaven passing away, dead not alive and living not dying, one becoming two. Leadership after Jesus departs goes to James the righteous, for whose sake heaven and earth came into being. Peter calls Jesus a righteous angel, Matthew a wise philosopher, Thomas cannot say whom he is like. Jesus takes Thomas aside and tells him three things Thomas will not repeat, for the companions would stone him and fire would come from the stones.

Sayings 14 to 22: fasting, prayer, and alms, if done as ritual, give rise to sin, condemnation, and harm to spirits; eat what is set before you, heal the sick, what goes into the mouth does not defile, what issues from it does. Worship the one not born of woman. Jesus came to cast dissension, fire, sword, and war, five in a house divided. What eye has not seen is given. Beginning and end are one place; blessed is he who takes his place in the beginning. Blessed is he who came into being before he came into being; five trees in Paradise whose leaves do not fall. The kingdom is like a mustard seed, like children in a field not theirs who undress and give it back, like a householder watching for a thief, arm yourselves against the world. Infants being suckled are like those who enter the kingdom; enter by making the two one, inside like outside, above like below, male and female one and the same, eye for eye, hand for hand, foot for foot, likeness for likeness.

Sayings 23 to 35: I shall choose you, one out of a thousand, two out of ten thousand, stand as a single one. Light within a man of light lights the whole world; without it he is darkness. Love your brother like your soul. Cast the beam from your own eye first. Fast as regards the world, observe the Sabbath as a Sabbath, or you will not find the kingdom or see the father. Jesus found the world intoxicated, blind, empty coming and empty leaving; when they shake off wine they will repent. Flesh from spirit is a wonder, spirit from body a wonder of wonders; great wealth has made its home in poverty. Where three gods, they are gods; where two or one, I am with him. No prophet accepted in his village, no physician heals those who know him. A city on a high mountain cannot fall or be hidden. Preach from housetops, lamp on a stand. A blind man leading a blind man falls in a pit. Bind the strong man first, then ransack his house. Do not be concerned morning to evening about what you will wear.

Sayings 36 to 50: disrobe without shame, tread garments under feet like little children, then see the son of the living one and not be afraid. Many times you desired to hear these words; days will come when you look for me and will not find me. Pharisees and scribes took the keys of knowledge and hidden them, entered not, nor allowed others; be wise as serpents, innocent as doves. A grapevine planted outside the father will be pulled up. Whoever has will receive more, whoever has nothing will be deprived of even the little he has. Become passers-by. You do not realize who I am; you have become like the Jews, loving the tree and hating its fruit, or the fruit and hating the tree. Blasphemy against father and son forgiven, against the holy spirit not forgiven on earth or in heaven. Grapes not from thorns, figs not from thistles; a good man brings forth good from his storehouse, an evil man evil from his heart. Among those born of women, none superior to John the Baptist that his eyes should not be lowered, yet whoever becomes a child will be acquainted with the kingdom and superior to John. Impossible to mount two horses, stretch two bows, serve two masters; old wine and new wine, old and new wineskins, old patch on new garment. If two make peace in one house, they will say to the mountain Move Away, and it will move. Blessed are the solitary and elect, you will find the kingdom, you are from it and to it return. If they ask where you came from, say from the light, we are its children, elect of the living father; the sign of your father in you is movement and repose.

Sayings 51 to 60: what you look forward to has already come, but you do not recognize it. Twenty-four prophets spoke in Israel, all spoke in you; you omitted the one living in your presence and spoke only of the dead. True circumcision in spirit is completely profitable, not the flesh. Blessed are the poor, yours is the kingdom of heaven. Whoever does not hate father and mother, brothers and sisters, and take up his cross in my way, cannot become my disciple and will not be worthy. Whoever understands the world has found only a corpse, and whoever has found a corpse is superior to the world. The kingdom is like good seed sown with weeds by night; do not pull the weeds lest you pull the wheat, at harvest they will be plainly visible, pulled up and burned. Blessed is the man who has suffered and found life. Take heed of the living one while alive, lest you die and seek to see him and be unable. Saying 60, in this rendering, is fragmentary, about a Samaritan carrying a lamb to Judea, the lamb eaten only when killed and become a corpse, and looking for a place within repose lest you become a corpse and be eaten.

Sayings 61 to 75: two will rest on a bed, one will die, the other live. Salome asks who Jesus is, come up on her couch and eaten from her table; he is from the undivided, given some of his father's things; if destroyed he will be filled with light, if divided filled with darkness. Mysteries are told to those worthy of them; do not let your left hand know what your right is doing. The rich man who planned to sow, reap, plant, and fill his storehouse died that same night. The dinner parable: guests excuse themselves for merchants, a house, a wedding banquet, a farm; the master sends the servant to the streets to bring back whom he meets; businessmen and merchants will not enter the places of my father. The vineyard tenants beat the servants and kill the son, the heir. Show me the stone the builders rejected, that one is the cornerstone. If one who knows the all still feels personal deficiency, he is completely deficient. Blessed when hated and persecuted, wherever persecuted they will find no place. Blessed persecuted within themselves, who truly know the father; blessed the hungry, the belly of him who desires will be filled. That which you have will save you if you bring it forth from yourselves; that which you do not have within you will kill you. I shall destroy this house, and no one will be able to build it. A man asks Jesus to divide his father's possessions; Jesus asks who made him a divider, and says he is not a divider. The harvest is great but the laborers are few. Many around the drinking trough, but nothing in the cistern. Many standing at the door, but the solitary will enter the bridal chamber.

Sayings 76 to 90: the kingdom is like a merchant who found a pearl, sold his merchandise and bought the pearl alone; seek unfailing treasure where no moth or worm destroys. It is I who am the light above them all, the all; from me the all came forth, unto me it extends; split wood and I am there, lift the stone and you will find me there. Why come into the desert, to see a reed shaken by wind, a man in fine garments like kings who cannot discern truth? A woman blesses the womb that bore Jesus and the breasts that nourished him; he answers blessed are those who heard the word of the father and kept it, for days will come when they say blessed are the womb that has not conceived and breasts that have not given milk. He who recognized the world has found the body, he who found the body is superior to the world. Let him who has grown rich be king, let him who possesses power renounce it. He who is near me is near the fire, far from me far from the kingdom. Images are manifest to man, but the light in them remains concealed in the image of the father's light; he will become manifest, but his image will remain concealed by his light. When you see your likeness you rejoice, but when you see your images which came into being before you, which neither die nor become manifest, how much you will have to bear. Adam came from great power and wealth but did not become worthy of you, for had he been worthy he would not have experienced death. Foxes have holes, birds have nests, the son of man has no place to lay his head. Wretched is the body dependent upon a body, and the soul dependent on these two. Angels and prophets will come and give you what you already have; give them what you have, and ask when they will come and take what is theirs. Why wash the outside of the cup? He who made the inside made the outside. Come unto me, my yoke is easy and my lordship mild, you will find repose.

Sayings 91 to 105: they ask Jesus to tell them who he is so they may believe; he says you read the face of sky and earth but have not recognized the one before you, and do not know how to read this moment. Seek and you will find; what you asked in former times and I did not tell you then, now I desire to tell, but you do not inquire. Saying 93 is fragmentary in this rendering: do not give what is holy to dogs, lest they throw them on the dung-heap; do not throw pearls to swine, lest they do something to it, the verb lost. He who seeks will find, he who knocks will be let in. If you have money, do not lend at interest, give to one from whom you will not get it back. The kingdom is like a woman who took leaven, concealed it in dough, and made large loaves. Like a woman carrying a jar of meal whose handle broke, the meal emptied on the road unnoticed, and she reached home with an empty jar. Like a man who wanted to kill a powerful man, drew his sword in his house and stuck it in the wall to test his hand, then slew him. Your brothers and mother stand outside; those who do the will of my father are my brothers and mother, they will enter the kingdom. Give Caesar what belongs to Caesar, God what belongs to God, and me what is mine. Whoever does not hate father and mother as I do cannot become a disciple, and whoever does not love father and mother as I do cannot become a disciple; my mother gave me life, but my true mother gave me life, the sentence partly lost. Woe to the pharisees, like a dog sleeping in the manger of oxen, neither eating nor letting the oxen eat. Fortunate is the man who knows where the brigands will enter, so he may get up, muster his domain, and arm himself before they invade. They say come, let us pray today and fast; Jesus asks what sin he committed or wherein defeated; when the bridegroom leaves the bridal chamber, then let them fast and pray. He who knows the father and the mother will be called the son of a harlot.

Sayings 106 to 114: when you make the two one, you will become the sons of man, and when you say Mountain, move away, it will move away. The kingdom is like a shepherd with a hundred sheep, one, the largest, went astray; he left the ninety-nine and looked for that one until he found it, and said I care for you more than the ninety-nine. He who will drink from my mouth will become like me, I myself shall become he, and the things hidden will be revealed to him. The kingdom is like a man with a hidden treasure in his field without knowing it; after he died his son inherited the field and sold it, the buyer plowed and found the treasure, and began to lend money at interest to whomever he wished. Whoever finds the world and becomes rich, let him renounce the world. The heavens and the earth will be rolled up in your presence; the one who lives from the living one will not see death. Does not Jesus say, whoever finds himself is superior to the world? Woe to the flesh that depends on the soul, woe to the soul that depends on the flesh. When will the kingdom come? It will not come by waiting, not by saying here it is or there it is. The kingdom of the father is spread out upon the earth, and men do not see it. Simon Peter says let Mary leave us, for women are not worthy of life. Jesus says I myself shall lead her in order to make her male, so that she too may become a living spirit resembling you males. For every woman who will make herself male will enter the kingdom of heaven.

Central thesis

Thomas does not tell a story about Jesus. It gives his secret sayings as a path to interpretation. The kingdom is not a future place or an institution. It is present, inside and outside, spread out on the earth, seen only by those who know themselves, become single instead of divided, and bring forth what is within them. The elect are solitary, chosen one out of a thousand, and they enter not by ritual, fasting, prayer, alms, circumcision, or lineage, but by becoming one, as children, as infants, as the undivided.

Key terms

Secret sayings: sayings given to those who can interpret them, not a public sermon. The All: the whole, the totality, which the finder will rule. The kingdom: present and hidden in plain sight, inside and outside. The solitary: the single, undivided person, also translated the elect. Movement and repose: the sign of the father in a person. Making the two one: male and female, inside and outside, above and below, brought into singleness. The living one: Jesus as living, not only as a teacher who died. Gnosis: knowledge, in saying 39 the keys of knowledge hidden by pharisees and scribes.

What the book does NOT prove

It does not prove these are Jesus's historical words, or that Thomas the apostle wrote them. It does not prove a date, an author, or a single community behind the collection. It does not prove the kingdom is only inward; the sayings also speak of the father, the light, the all, and a coming end, and they leave much of that unresolved. It does not prove women are excluded; saying 114 is the hardest passage in the book, and it is presented here as written, not smoothed over. The lacunae in sayings 60, 61, 93, and 101 are gaps in the manuscript as rendered here, not places where I filled in the missing words.

Questions to carry forward

Why does Thomas keep so many canonical parallels, sower, mustard seed, vineyard, pearl, lost sheep, yet remove the narrative, the passion, and the resurrection? What does making the two one mean in practice, and how is it different from simply ignoring the body? How should saying 114 be read, as a statement about gender, as a metaphor for singleness, or as a later addition? If the kingdom has already come and is spread out on the earth, what, if anything, is still to come?
